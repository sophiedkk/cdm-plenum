Before you go on to Decide

Discover runs in four parts: start from what exists, add what is missing, clean up, then group. Each item opens the page where you do it.

  1. Start from existing models. 3 models imported: 14 elements on the list. Review
  2. Participants go through the list. 6 of 8 done.
  3. Add what is missing. 5 of 8 added elements, 9 new elements, hidden from each other. Open the board
  4. Reveal and clean up. Reveal the board, then merge the duplicates people marked.
  5. Group the elements. You or a modeller put each element in a group; participants check it. Group
